    // ABOUT AETHERION VOYAGES

    Thirteen Years Beyond Earth

    Aetherion Voyages was founded in 2087 by a small crew of ex-orbital pilots who believed Mars tourism shouldn't mean a shared bunk and a ration pack. We chartered the first privately licensed Earth–Mars luxury liner route two years later, and have spent every year since building the resorts, vessels, and AI systems that make the six-month crossing feel like the best part of the trip, not something to endure before it.

    Today our fleet ferries travellers to six territories across the Martian surface and orbit, backed by a dual AI concierge system — ARIA for your journey, TERRA for your connection home — and a safety record we publish in full, not just the parts that flatter us. See our current numbers in the Mission Risk Intelligence panel; we don't believe in luxury that hides its risks.
